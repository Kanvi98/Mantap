How are you travelling?

By BMTC bus from Majestic

258C258CCother 258-series to Nelamangala
  1. Board at Kempegowda Bus Station (Majestic)

    Take a BMTC bus whose board reads Nelamangala Bus Station. It runs via Malleshwaram, Yeshwanthpur, Peenya and Jalahalli Cross. Ride time is about 1 hr 20 min, more in traffic.

  2. Get down at Weavers Colony, Nelamangala

    Stops in order near the end: Binnamangala → Nursery → Weavers Colony → TB. Ask the conductor for "Weavers Colony".

  3. Walk to the mantapa

    Stay on the same side of the road you got down on. Walk back the way the bus came (towards Bengaluru) for about 240 m, then turn right into the lane and walk about 90 m. The mantapa is on that lane.

By Namma Metro, then bus

Green LineMajestic → Madavara16 stops
  1. Board the Green Line at Majestic

    At Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station (Majestic), go to the Green Line platform for trains towards Madavara.

  2. Get down at Madavara, the last station

    The train passes Sampige Road, Rajajinagar, Yeshwanthpur, Peenya, Jalahalli, Nagasandra and Chikkabidarakallu. Madavara is the end of the line, next to BIEC.

  3. Take a bus towards Nelamangala

    Exit to Tumkur Road (NH-48) and use the bus stop on the Nelamangala-bound side. Board 258C, 258CC or any BMTC bus going to Nelamangala Bus Station. About 30 min.

  4. Get down at Weavers Colony, Nelamangala

    Same stop as the bus route: after Binnamangala and Nursery.

  5. Walk to the mantapa

    Stay on the same side of the road. Walk back towards Bengaluru about 240 m, turn right into the lane, then about 90 m to the mantapa.
